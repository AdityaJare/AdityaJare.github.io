About Aditya Jare

AI/ML developer engineering data-centric machine learning pipelines, deep learning architectures, and actively preparing for a Master's degree in Computer Vision.

Professional Biography

I am an AI/ML developer passionate about turning theoretical machine learning algorithms into robust, deployed applications. My engineering philosophy revolves around empirical validation: auditing for insidious data leakage, addressing severe real-world class imbalances, and rigorously benchmarking traditional ensembles against deep neural networks.

My development portfolio spans end-to-end regression pipelines (such as StrengthCast, achieving an R² of 0.93 with XGBoost and serialized with Joblib), complex 22-class classification architectures, and multi-output transfer learning convolutional neural networks with Keras Functional API.

Core Academic Goal Target: Master's in Computer Vision

The Road to a Master's in Computer Vision

My long-term aspiration is to pursue an intensive Master of Science in Computer Vision at a premier international research institution. Having established strong foundations in classical ML and CNN backbones (MobileNetV2, VGG16), I am actively expanding into advanced vision domains to prepare for graduate-level research.

Key Topics Currently Under Study:

1. Object Detection

Two-stage (Faster R-CNN) and single-stage detectors (YOLOv8/v9), IoU loss functions, anchor-free feature pyramids.

2. Semantic & Instance Segmentation

U-Net, Mask R-CNN, and pixel-level classification architectures for biomedical and autonomous vehicle perception.

3. PyTorch Deep Learning

Custom autograd modules, distributed data parallel training, torchvision transforms, and PyTorch Lightning pipelines.

4. Vision Transformers (ViT)

Patch embeddings, multi-head self-attention mechanisms, and hybrid CNN-Transformer backbones.
