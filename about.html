Professional Biography

I am a B.E. graduate in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning (AIML) passionate about turning theoretical machine learning algorithms into robust, deployed applications. My engineering philosophy revolves around empirical validation: auditing for insidious data leakage, addressing severe real-world class imbalances, and rigorously benchmarking traditional ensembles against deep neural networks.

My development journey spans classical ML, ANN, CNN, and extends into RNN, Generative AI (GenAI), and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG). Above all, I hold a deep fascination and focused dedication for Computer Vision tasks—such as object detection, image segmentation, and vision transformers. Alongside my technical pursuits, I am dedicated to my Japanese language journey (targeting JLPT N3) to engage with international technology ecosystems.

Computer Vision Specialization & Advanced AI

As a B.E. graduate in AI & ML, I continuously explore advanced ML, ANN, CNN, RNN, GenAI, and RAG architectures. Among all domains, I am most passionately drawn to Computer Vision tasks—engineering visual models that perceive, interpret, and process visual data with high fidelity and low latency.

Key Vision & AI Domains Under Focus:

1. Object Detection

Two-stage (Faster R-CNN) and single-stage detectors (YOLOv8/v9), IoU loss functions, and anchor-free feature pyramids.

2. Semantic & Instance Segmentation

U-Net, Mask R-CNN, and pixel-level classification architectures for biomedical and autonomous perception.

3. Vision Transformers (ViT) & GenAI

Patch embeddings, self-attention mechanisms, diffusion pipelines, multi-modal embeddings, and vision-RAG integration.

4. Deep Learning Architectures (ANN/CNN/RNN)

Custom PyTorch & Keras networks, sequential modeling with RNN/LSTMs, transfer learning backbones, and inference optimization.
