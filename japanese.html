🏆 1st Place Victory: Our team brought home #1 Best Project at the inaugural Bunkasai 2026 Japanese Cultural Festival in Pune. Batches Fushimi and Akagi joined hands to build an interactive cultural showcase celebrating Japanese folklore and traditions.

🎋 Immersive Cultural Booths: Our centerpiece booth, "Unmei to Negai no Monogatari" (Story of Fate & Wishes), brought traditional festivals to life with authentic Tanabata wish-making, Daruma goal determination ceremonies, hand-drawn Omikuji fortune slips, and Juunishi zodiac lore.

💬 "One of the most unforgettable milestones for me was conversing live and entirely in Japanese with native Japanese visitors, explaining the historical significance of Tanabata. Putting classroom learning into spontaneous practice in real time was priceless — that is what this language journey is all about!"
