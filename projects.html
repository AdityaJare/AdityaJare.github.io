JURI-AI: AI-Powered Legal Assistant Platform

A comprehensive AI-driven legal intelligence platform built with React, Node.js/Express, FastAPI, and MongoDB. Powered by Google Gemini AI and Pinecone vector search to deliver instant statutory advice, automated contract risk analysis, and provide legal document .

01 / 13
🤖

Core AI Capabilities

  • Statutory AI Assistant: Context-grounded natural language queries powered by Google Gemini AI & Pinecone Vector DB with statutory law citations.
  • Contract Risk Analysis: Automated clause-by-clause grading (High/Medium/Low risk), FEMA 1999 & consumer act compliance, and legal safety alerts.
  • Smart Template System: Pre-built legal contracts with category filters, and instant downloads.
  • Multi-Role RBAC: Dedicated portals with role-based access for Users, verified Lawyers (consultation chat), and Admins.
